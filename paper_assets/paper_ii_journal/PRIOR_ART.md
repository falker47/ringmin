## Sources and Search Boundary

- [Paper I public v2 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28654) identifies
  the September 15 corrective version. The local archived v2 source was read
  for the model, claims and asymptotic correction; v1 is historical only.
- [Paper II preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13630) and the protected
  local TeX identify the original contribution; no public file is revised.
- [Alt et al., journal record](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/article/view/3056),
  DOI [10.20382/jocg.v9i1a10](https://doi.org/10.20382/jocg.v9i1a10);
  [author preprint full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.01239v2).
  The journal PDF endpoint redirected unreliably; the primary preprint
  provides the readable full text. The shelf envelope differs from twice
  the line span by at most 2 for marks <=1, so bounded endpoints disappear
  after normalization, but do not prove our limit or recovery.
- [Dechter, Meiri and Pearl, original article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370291900066),
  DOI [10.1016/0004-3702(91)90006-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90006-6).
  Used only to attribute difference constraints, not as an asymptotic premise.

Queries covered these exact titles, JoCG official requirements and directly
related shelf/difference-constraint models. No systematic database search,
citation-graph exhaustion or universal priority claim was performed.
Editorial significance remains a referee/editor judgment.

Additional directly relevant primary source identified through Alt et al.:
[Klemz, Noellenburg and Prutkin, author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.00720),
[DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-27261-0_36](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-27261-0_36).

from Ringmin's uniform outer radii and allowed additional tangencies. This source
is now cited in the candidate introduction. No broad citation-graph review was run.
