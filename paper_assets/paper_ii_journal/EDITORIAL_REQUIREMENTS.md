# Editorial Requirements and Target Decision

JoCG remains a candidate venue. Requirements were retrieved from official
[submission instructions](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/about/submissions)
and their [Carleton-hosted mirror](https://ojs.library.carleton.ca/index.php/jocg/about/submissions)
on 2026-10-23. Indexed full text was accessible; some direct requests produced
redirect loops. The two retrieved versions agreed on the applicable checklist.

| Review-stage requirement | Candidate handling |
|---|---|
| LaTeX, 11 or 12 pt, single spacing, PDF | Standard 11 pt article, default single spacing |
| Page and line numbers | Main and S2 use page numbers and lineno; S1 remains the unchanged indexed standalone supplement |
| DOI where available; title-case headings | Main bibliography and headings updated |
| Supporting supplementary files | Self-contained source/check archive and two indexed supplements |
| Portal abstract identical to paper | No portal action; main.tex is the future abstract source |
| Publication/parallel-submission disclosure | Preprints disclosed; related Paper I submission identified in PRIOR_ART.md |

Camera-ready instructions are different: jocg.cls, US letter, no page/line
numbers, no contents list and plainurl bibliography. They apply after acceptance;
the candidate uses review formatting, with US letter already chosen.
No acceptance-triggered conversion or portal declaration is performed.

The official [review criteria](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/about/review-criteria)
emphasize geometric relevance, conceptual/technical contribution and interest.
This candidate foregrounds the effective geometric limit, quantifies recovery
and distinguishes known shelf machinery. That is a positioning argument, not
a prediction of editorial acceptance.

Future venue choice, author metadata confirmation and actual submission are
outside this task. No paid service, publication or submission was created.
