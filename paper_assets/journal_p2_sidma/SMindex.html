Title of paper: The minimum central circle for prescribed radius profiles

Authors: Maurizio Falconi

File: supplement.pdf

Type: PDF

Contents: S1 gives the full proof of the lower endpoint for consecutive integer radii. It derives the eight steps summarized in the article and gives the rational enclosures used in the proof.

File: s1_support.zip

Type: ZIP archive of source, code and verification records

Contents: The original S1 package contains the arithmetic scripts, certificate and manifest. To reproduce the recorded checks, extract the archive and run python -I -S verify.py in lower_supplement/. The original source and PDF are inputs to the manifest check.
