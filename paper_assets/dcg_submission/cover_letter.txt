Dear Editors-in-Chief,

Please consider my manuscript, "Minimum central circles: chain order, seam
obstructions, and exact finite certification", for publication in Discrete &
Computational Geometry.

The paper gives a standalone finite treatment of the minimum central circle
externally tangent to pairwise non-overlapping circles of radii 1,2,...,n.
It identifies a strict anti-Monge angular kernel and applies Supnick's
classical tour theorem to the adjacent-chain relaxation, with a separate
argument transferring the minimizing order to the chain-radius problem.
A self-contained seam theorem characterizes full feasibility of the canonical
Supnick necklace at its chain root, proves eventual persistent failure for
each fixed starting radius, and gives the complete classifications for
starting radii 1, 2 and 3. For the global geometric problem, exact-arithmetic
certificates establish rational brackets L_n < R*(n) <= U_n of width 10^-11
for every 3 <= n <= 14. These brackets distinguish global certification
from chain optimality and numerical observations about floating circles.

The geometric feasibility questions and their interaction with combinatorial
optimization motivate submission to Discrete & Computational Geometry.
The proofs needed for the seam result are included in the manuscript.
The reproducible software and certificate archive is available on Zenodo at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22849826, with the computational source pin and
the archive's precise scope stated in the paper. The finite certification
does not rely on floating-point pruning in the historical search.

An earlier finite version is publicly available as arXiv:2607.28654v2.
The separate asymptotic sequel, "Minimum central circles: an effective
characterization of the global asymptotic constant", is available as
arXiv:2609.13630. The present submission is self-contained and does not
depend on the sequel's asymptotic results.
