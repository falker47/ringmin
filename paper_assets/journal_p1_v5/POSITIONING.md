# Positioning for the Journal of Global Optimization

For prescribed outer radii, minimizing the radius of a common tangent circle requires choosing both a cyclic order and a placement. This paper gives an exact certification method for any finite list of at least three distinct positive integer radii. It supplies rational brackets of width 10⁻¹¹ for both the linear and quadratic families at every size from 3 through 14. Integer dynamic programming and angular inequalities cover all cyclic orders at the lower endpoint. Rational Cartesian witnesses establish feasibility at the upper endpoint. The anti-Monge kernel identifies a tractable chain relaxation for arbitrary distinct positive radii. For consecutive integer radii a seam condition decides whether the canonical chain is geometrically feasible at its root. Readers can replay both finite families with a standalone verifier.
