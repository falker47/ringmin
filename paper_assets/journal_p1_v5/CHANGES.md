# Editorial changes for the Paper I journal draft

This Journal of Global Optimization draft builds on arXiv:2607.28654v4 and the journal adaptation in PR #7. The v4 source archive has SHA-256 `e5d499657a47cea462fcaedae78f2dab1eb69f594315cd91aa9a61c71b5081ab`. The general-radii formulation, second linear audit and quadratic certificates come from [PR #9](https://github.com/falker47/ringmin/pull/9).

| Change | Purpose and check |
| --- | --- |
| Title, abstract and introduction | “Exact global bounds for minimum central circles with prescribed integer radii” presents the method for any finite list of at least three distinct positive integers. The two computed families and their range are explicit. The abstract contains 157 whitespace-delimited words. |
| Related work | Sixteen cited records have DOI checks, including the six original references. Equal-circle packings in a square, unit squares in a circle, shelf packing, contact graphs and exact computation explain the relationship to global optimization. All bibliographic links are active DOI links in the PDF. |
| Structural results | The adjacent-chain theorem and the seam theorem now lead from the geometric relaxation to finite global exclusion. Every original quantified statement is retained. The complete seam proof and numerical table inputs are transcribed from v4. |
| Six figures | Each panel has a caption and source. Figures 2 and 3 show the transition from a feasible closing chain to a forced overlap. Figure 4 highlights the two disks from the historical numerical description and independently checks all strict distances at the upper endpoint. |
| Quadratic radii | The new main-text section prints all twelve exact quadratic brackets from PR #9. The TeX table is copied into the standalone journal source bundle and checked against its source and the individual certificate files. Appendix C proves the deletion/insertion count and the positive rational buffer for any allowed list. |
| Independent linear reproduction | Section 6 identifies the published endpoints and previous witness orders supplied to the second generator. It recomputes coverage and rational placements. The independent checker takes one new-format certificate per case. |
| Finite and asymptotic results | Figure 5 shows the twelve normalized brackets in each family, the interval for the linear limiting coefficient and the upper line for the quadratic limit. Both numerical references concern the limiting constants. |
| Scope paragraph | The final discussion distinguishes an individual reconstruction, existence of slack at an optimum, and universal contact claims. Quantifiers in the formal results and in the reconstruction table stay with the claims. |
| Reproduction details | Section 10 distinguishes the original pinned verifier and Zenodo release from the general-radii archive. It gives both isolated replay commands, the certificate paths and the general-radii source revision. References precede the appendices. |
| Author and declarations | The original author, affiliation, contact, manuscript date, and Statements and Declarations (including the AI-use paragraph) are preserved. |
